GPT5 Marketing brand

Logo

The mark is a single path that forks into a fast branch and a deep branch and rejoins at one point: many lines of reasoning, one accountable decision. Download: logo.svg · logo-dark.svg · mark.svg

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark on every side.
  • Minimum size: 24 px tall on screen. Use the mark alone below 120 px wide.
  • Never recolour, stretch, outline or add effects. Use the dark version on #0A0A0F and photography.
  • Pair with the SOMIN partner lockup only as “Technology partner SOMIN”, never merged into the GPT5 Marketing logo.

Colour

Accent is for actions, highlights and data. Body text on light surfaces uses #2A2A35; links use #4F46E5 to keep AA contrast.

We redesign marketing around reasoning models, grounded in real signal

An independent frontier AI strategy studio for CMOs. We rebuild strategy, creative, research and measurement workflows for the reasoning era, with SOMIN as the evidence layer underneath.

Personality

  • rigorous
  • clear-eyed
  • curious
  • calm

Voice: do

  • Name the trade-off and the evidence behind every claim
  • Explain mechanisms plainly, with worked examples
  • State what is uncertain and what stays human

Voice: don't

  • Hype models or promise magic results
  • Claim affiliation with OpenAI or any model vendor
  • Invent statistics, clients or quotes

Imagery

Near-black fields with iridescent indigo-to-pink holographic glass and branching reasoning paths. Abstract, editorial, luminous; never robots, screens of code or stock handshakes.

All imagery is generated with SOMIN image generation from art-directed prompts: no stock, no text baked into images, no identifiable real people.
