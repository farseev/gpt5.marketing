# GPT5 Marketing brand guidelines (v1.0, October 2026)

**Positioning:** The frontier AI strategy studio that redesigns marketing workflows around reasoning models and grounds them in real market signal.
**Tagline:** Marketing redesigned for reasoning models
**Domain / contact:** gpt5.marketing · ask@gpt5.marketing
**Technology partner:** SOMIN (somin.ai). Show as "Technology partner SOMIN" with the two-tone SOMIN wordmark (SO teal #2DBF9C, MIN dark/white), never merged into our logo.

## Logo
The mark is a single path that forks into a fast branch and a deep branch and rejoins at one point: many lines of reasoning, one accountable decision.
Files: `brand/logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `brand/logo-dark.svg` (dark backgrounds), `brand/mark.svg` (icon), `favicon.svg`.
Clear space = height of the mark. Minimum 24 px tall. No recolouring, stretching, outlines or effects.

## Typography
- Headlines: Manrope 800, tracking -0.035em, sentence case
- Body: Manrope 400/500, 17 px / 1.65
- Labels: Manrope 600, 12–13 px, uppercase, 0.2em tracking, accent colour
- Corner radius: 20px

## Personality
- rigorous
- clear-eyed
- curious
- calm

## Voice
Do:
- Name the trade-off and the evidence behind every claim
- Explain mechanisms plainly, with worked examples
- State what is uncertain and what stays human

Don't:
- Hype models or promise magic results
- Claim affiliation with OpenAI or any model vendor
- Invent statistics, clients or quotes

## Imagery
Near-black fields with iridescent indigo-to-pink holographic glass and branching reasoning paths. Abstract, editorial, luminous; never robots, screens of code or stock handshakes. All imagery generated with SOMIN image generation; no text in images, no stock, no identifiable real people.

## Calls to action
Every primary CTA ("Start a frontier sprint") opens an email to ask@gpt5.marketing.
